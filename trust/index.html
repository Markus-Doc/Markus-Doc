Why you can trust us

Check our work yourself.

Every finding our checks use is open to read, with the vendor's own words, the source link and the date we checked it. Anyone can confirm a result without taking our word for it, and we publish what people find.

What you can check

  • The knowledge base. 22,448 findings across 488 AI platforms and tools and 46 security controls, each with its sources and the date checked. The free AI Stack Check uses 114 of these platforms.
  • Open problems. Critical and high controls almost no platform documents yet, with our definitions and when each last changed.
  • How it works. How a finding is researched, checked and approved.
  • AU Frameworks Check. Each of its 219 questions names the requirement it tests, quoting the framework where its licence allows and linking to it where not.

How a finding is checked. Agents research it from the vendor's public documentation. With no vendor quote, the control is recorded as not covered.

A freshness check fetches the page again, a validity check quotes it, a second model tries to prove the finding wrong and code confirms the quote is on the page. Human experts on our team then approve it.

Agents look for vendor changes daily, and a changed finding needs human approval again. No vendor sponsors the check or can change a finding.

How to check a finding yourself

  1. Open the knowledge base and search for the platform.
  2. Select the mark for your control to open the finding, the source link and the date checked.
  3. Follow the link and find the quoted passage. If the page has changed since, tell us.
  4. Compare it with your setup. Whether it is switched on in your account is yours to confirm.

Tell us when we are wrong

If a finding is wrong, out of date or too strict, report a finding with the platform, the control and the page that shows it. The form prepares an email and sends nothing until you send it. Vendors can use it too.

A correction passes the same checks as any finding. If you want credit, we name you in the record below.

    What we do not claim

    • Our checks show readiness. They do not certify you or find you compliant, and they are not an audit or legal advice.
    • A finding describes what a vendor documents. Vendor documentation does not prove how a platform is set up in your account.
    • The AU Frameworks Check draws on research that is still under review and on your own answers, which we do not check.
