Why you can trust us

Check our work yourself.

We give you our judgement and the evidence behind it. Every finding our checks use is open to read, with the vendor's own words, a link to the source and the date we checked it. Anyone, at any technical level, can confirm a result without taking our word for it.

Human confirmation is the step AI cannot complete on its own. The more people check our findings, the more of that confirmation there is, and we publish what they find.

What you can check

  • The knowledge base. 22,448 findings across 488 AI platforms and tools and 46 security controls. Select any cell for the finding, its sources and the date we checked it. The free AI Stack Check uses 114 of these platforms.
  • Open problems. The critical and high controls almost no platform documents yet, how we define each one and when we last changed a definition.
  • How it works. How a finding is researched, checked and approved, and how the records connect.
  • AU Frameworks Check. Each of its 219 questions names the framework requirement it tests. Results quote the framework's wording where its licence allows, and link to the source where it does not.

How a finding is checked. Agents research it from the vendor's public documentation. If we cannot quote the vendor, the control is recorded as not covered.

A freshness check fetches the vendor's page again, a validity check quotes it, and a second model tries to prove the finding wrong. Code confirms the quote is on the page. Human experts on our team then approve it.

Agents look for vendor changes every day. A finding that changes waits for human approval again. No vendor sponsors the check or can change a finding.

How to check a finding yourself

  1. Open the knowledge base and type the platform's name in Find.
  2. Select the mark for the control you care about. The finding opens with the vendor's own words, the source link and the date we checked it.
  3. Follow the link and find the quoted passage on the vendor's page. If the page has changed since that date, tell us.
  4. Compare it with your own setup. A finding says what the vendor documents. Whether it is switched on in your account is for you to confirm.

Tell us when we are wrong

If a finding is wrong, out of date or defined too strictly, email markus@markuswalker.com with the platform, the control and the page that shows it. Vendors can send their own documentation the same way. You can also write to us from the enquiry form, which prepares an email and sends nothing until you send it.

A correction passes the same checks as any finding before it changes the knowledge base. If you want credit, we name you in the corrections record below.

    What we do not claim

    • Our checks show readiness. They do not certify you or find you compliant, and they are not an audit or legal advice.
    • A finding describes what a vendor documents. Vendor documentation does not prove how a platform is set up in your account.
    • The AU Frameworks Check draws on research that is still under review and on your own answers, which we do not check.
