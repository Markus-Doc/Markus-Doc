  • Lane A, the manuscript world: in light the network is drawn on worked parchment with marginalia; four approved ink studies (book tree, escapement, compass, quills) sit on panels round the brain; in dark they appear on lamp-lit parchment plates.
  • Lane B, engraved instruments: small modelled brass instruments at the section hubs (quadrant, dividers, astrolabe, sector, compass rose, nocturnal), shown only where they have a clear spot.
  • Night skin: a far focus band (sharp through the brain, soft in front and behind) for your flowing-mesh reference.
  • Every project and document page from the static site now lives inside the site at /projects/<slug>/; the 24 September resume (no phone number) ships.
  • Sweep fixes: tags and chips clear the map controls, no wrong first frame, the whole-network glide no longer empties the scene, no gold beam at dark arrivals, phone controls hidden during the dive, phone Index anchored, tags clear hub rings.
