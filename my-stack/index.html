One list for every tool

List your AI stack once. Every tool picks it up.

Add the AI platforms and tools your business uses, answer a few shared questions, and the AI Stack Check, AU Frameworks Check, stack pack, AI register and shadow AI check all start from it. Edit it as your AI use grows or shrinks and see what changes.

Your list stays in your browser unless you create an account and save it there. It holds only what you pick here: platform names, banded answers and a short optional label. No results, no names of people, no secrets.
