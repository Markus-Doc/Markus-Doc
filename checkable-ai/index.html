How we think

Three beliefs shape every piece of work we take on.

Build for the AI that is coming

Models get better every few months. We put effort into what stays valuable however capable they become: your own knowledge, decisions and evidence, kept in a form you own.

No lock-in to one AI engine

Nobody knows which AI tools will last. We design with the engine as a swappable part, using open formats and standard protocols, so you can change models without starting again.

Plain words, real evidence, work that gets done

No jargon and no long workshops for their own sake. Every finding we give you comes with its source, and every engagement ends with something you can use.
