Interactive capstone

Ask the Atlas: an interactive questionnaire over the knowledge base

October 2026.

  • Try it →
  • The Offerings Atlas case study →
  • View the public sample ↗

The capstone of the Offerings Atlas, and the part you can try. Tell it where you are with AI agents, what you run and what worries you, and watch it walk the knowledge base to an answer in which every citation is checked against the record it came from.

It runs entirely in your browser over a select public sample of the atlas: 14 of its 151 platforms and tools across all 44 controls, 610 records with their sources, and the evaluator proof for the verified cells. The sample is kept small on purpose so the demonstration stays light, and I plan to release the full atlas as open source in time. Each concern is routed to the controls it is about, the records for your stack are read from the OKF tree, alternatives are found where your stack falls short, and the answer is framed by your maturity stage and the engagements that fit it.

What it shows

  • Visible traversal. A graph and a step log show every hop: concern to control, control to the record for each platform, record to the sources it cites.
  • Checked citations. Every claim names its record and source, and the check against the record shows beside it.
  • Provenance on tap. Open any record to see its verdict, gaps, sources, OKF path and, for verified cells, the quoted passage with its page hash.
  • Honest labels. Agent verified, owner accepted and outside the sample are shown as different things.
  • The full version beside it. The model-written Client A run, routed by Haiku and answered by Sonnet through my local gateway, plays back with its own traversal.

The live version routes by keyword and assembles answers from records without a model, so it costs nothing to run and nothing you type leaves the page. The atlas itself is closed source for now. Verdicts describe documented capability, not tested deployments.
