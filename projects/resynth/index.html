RESYNTH research pipeline

First released June 2026, last updated July 2026.

A command-line pipeline that takes several AI deep-research reports on one topic and consolidates them into a single verified master document.

  • View repository ↗

It follows systematic review methods. Every claim keeps its source, conflicts are handled in the open, and completeness is checked by machine.

The pipeline has no runtime AI dependency. The thinking is supplied by whichever AI assistant drives it from outside, and every stage can be rerun and inspected from the shell with file-backed state.

How it runs

  • Installs on Windows, macOS and Linux, with a guided mode for first use
  • Detects Claude Code, Codex or Gemini on the machine and can wire one in to do the thinking steps
  • Writes one tailored research prompt per platform, then loads and fingerprints the reports that come back
  • Extracts and compares claims, then drives the consolidation into the master document

MIT licensed. Parts of it may since have been overtaken by newer tooling.
