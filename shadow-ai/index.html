Shadow AI self-check for Australian businesses

See how your staff use AI outside approved tools, and what to do first.

17 plain questions, about five minutes. You get:

  • an exposure level (low, moderate or high) with the reasons
  • the risks, such as personal accounts that can train on chats by default, data stored outside Australia, and Privacy Act APP 8 and APP 11
  • a 30 and 90 day action list: approved tools, business accounts, a policy, training and a register
  • account types and training defaults for the platforms in your saved stack

Your answers never leave your browser.

A self-check from your own answers. It cannot see what staff do, and it is not legal advice.

No sign-up needed.

What Australian surveys found

  • 56%of Australian workers who use AI at work rely on tools their employer did not provide or approve. Salesforce survey run by YouGov, 1,293 Australians. IT Brief, 22 June 2026
  • 70%of Australian office workers surveyed regularly use AI despite policies restricting it. PagerDuty with Wakefield Research. HR Leader, 15 July 2026

How it works

  1. Five short steps: your business, what staff use, what goes in, your rules, and how you would know.
  2. Written rules turn answers into risks, each citing the OAIC, the Privacy Act, the national Guidance for AI Adoption, the AICD checklist for SME directors, a vendor's terms or our knowledge base.

Automatic discovery tools need software on devices or the network. This check needs nothing installed and shows whether one is worth it.

Your answers stay in this page unless you pick a save option in the stack bar. Saved answers are list picks, never free text.
