AI SDLC self-check

How does your team build and change AI features?

Fifteen questions for teams that build on models, mapped to NIST SP 800-218A and the OWASP AI Maturity Assessment. You get the gaps and a 30 and 90 day plan.

Your answers never leave your browser unless you sign in and choose to save them to your account.

This check is for teams that build on models, such as prompts, RAG, fine-tunes or agents. You can still run it.

A self-check, not an audit or a certification. References checked against NIST SP 800-218A (July 2024) and OWASP AIMA v1.0 (August 2025) on 22 October 2026.
